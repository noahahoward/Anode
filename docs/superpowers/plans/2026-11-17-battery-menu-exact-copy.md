## Reuse from the Sound menu

`SystemPanelKind.battery` and the press driver already exist (they read `energy-mode-*`). Reuse: the glass material, `HoverRow`, `BadgeView`, the glide between layouts, `CoverPresence`/`IconCover`, `NativeIconClickGate`, and the measuring tools (recorder, bands, lens profile) in the session scratchpad.

## Tasks

1. **Metrics, model, layout** (PowerKit, pure): `BatteryMenuMetrics`, `BatteryMenuModel` (Apple's reading: power source line, energy modes with selected, significant-energy apps), `BatteryMenuLayout.frames(for:)`; tests pin the numbers above, with and without the significant-energy section.
2. **Reading**: `AppleBatteryPanel` parsed from the Control Center tree (AX ids above), with tests from recorded trees.
3. **Controller**: opens from a click on macOS's Battery icon (the icon gate made generic over the item id), reads Apple's panel off-screen first, presses `energy-mode-*` through the driver, optimistic selection, the same open/close rules as Sound.
4. **Panel view**: draws the rows from the layout (glass, fonts, glyphs, hover, glide as rows come and go).
5. **Setting**: a Beta Features switch "Battery menu" (off by default; the Sound menu's pattern).
6. **Verification against Apple**: overlay over flat backdrops, hover, press, animation, light appearance; then the additions of step 4.

- 2026-11-17 (owner: find the least %/hr at which macOS lists an app under "Using Significant Energy", and use it as Anode's own minimum) Method: a test app whose load is set from a file, in ~/Applications; macOS's list read unseen every ~16 s (Accessibility, panel moved off screen at once); Anode's own watts for the app read from `history.sqlite` (`app_energy` joules / `interval.dur`; 1 % of this battery is about 2,700 J, so %/hr is about 1.33 x watts). Findings: (1) a windowed app hidden behind others is App Nap'd and draws a fraction of the load asked for (measure it, do not trust the duty); (2) the rule is POWER, not CPU time: 0.46 cores of work on one fast core (about 1.0 W) is listed, the same 0.46 cores spread over 2 or 4 threads (about 0.08 W) is not; (3) steady power listed: 0.55 to 1.2 W (0.73 to 1.6 %/hr), always within 66 to 130 s of reaching it; not listed in 2.5 min at 0.25 W (0.33 %/hr) or less; so the cut-off is between about 0.3 and 0.55 W, i.e. 0.4 to 0.73 %/hr, about 0.5 %/hr in the middle; (4) it leaves the list 82 to 162 s after the load stops (listing is on a rolling window of a minute or two, not the instant); (5) VS Code was listed at a trailing 0.40 W, Anode at 0.4 to 0.7 W; (6) background agents (LSUIElement test apps, no window) were never listed, even at 3.9, 8 and 20 W for 25 min each, while real apps with a window were. Suggested rule for Anode's list: apps (identity isApp) at or above 0.5 %/hr, kept until they fall under 0.35 %/hr, at most a handful, sorted by %/hr; not built yet (the owner asked for it as a bug fix after the release; they are updating macOS first, which will end every app, so this note is the record). Test app removed from ~/Applications; scripts in the session scratchpad `burn/` (burn.swift, battlist.swift, ramp.sh).
